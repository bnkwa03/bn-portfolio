Innovation Lab · Internal Tool

Lenses — a deck-review copilot

A Chrome extension that reviews any in-progress deck against the criteria that matter to the person using it — design, story arc, audience fit, and alignment with Thresh’s brand guidelines.

Role
End-to-end — research, product design, and engineering (solo)

The problem

Auditing client decks for audience fit and alignment with Thresh’s brand guidelines was slow and manual — feedback took time to gather and was easy to miss. I scoped that inefficiency, then designed and built the tool to close it.

What it does

Lenses reads a deck in place and returns per-slide notes you can accept or decline, each tuned to the criteria relevant to whoever’s reviewing. Because it’s internal, it stays grounded in Thresh’s own materials.

End‑to‑end

Scoped, designed, built

From needfinding through the full design and engineering of the extension.

Team‑wide

Made for everyone reviewing

Product designers, PMs, and growth leads — cutting the time spent waiting on feedback.

Live

Always current

Connected to Thresh materials on the backend; updated from standups, meetings, Slack, and docs.

What it reviews

Design Review

Brand-guideline and voice checks, slide by slide.

Story Arc

Whether the deck’s narrative holds together end to end.

Audience & Context Fit

Whether the framing matches who it’s actually for.

Inconsistencies

Contradictions and drift flagged against the knowledge base.
