iFind

Usability research and research ops for an AI tool that turns hour-long interaction videos into structured coaching clips.

The problem

FIND coaches caregivers from 60-minute interaction videos. Turning each one into a structured coaching clip meant manual annotation and splicing across three people. iFind automates that with AI. My job was to make sure the beta tool was actually usable, and to keep research moving while engineering caught up.

Methodology

01

30 moderated think-aloud sessions

Think-aloud surfaced live mental models and the exact moments the AI-assisted flow lost people, not just that it did. I screened for novice and experienced editors to see where habits diverged.

02

Standardized research ops

A shared repository, scheduling system, and note format kept findings consistent and comparable across facilitators, so quality did not drift session to session.

03

A living prototype for engineering

Text-only notes forced engineers to interpret each change, which slowed builds. Pairing insights with an interactive Figma prototype spelled out every interaction and removed the guesswork.

The iFind editor, recreated from a mockup (the production tool is under NDA).

Impact

12→4 users hitting the same unfixed build, once a prototype rode alongside the notes.
  • Ran 30 moderated usability sessions end to end.
  • Shipped changes that gave users control over AI suggestions, a definitions cheat sheet that ended conceptual confusion, and an editing flow rebuilt around familiar patterns.
  • Stood up the team’s research repository, scheduling, and note standards.
