Thresh Consulting · Internal Tool

Lenses

A deck-review copilot that catches what’s off in a deck before it ever ships.

Reviewing client decks for audience fit and alignment with Thresh’s brand guidelines was slow and manual — feedback took days and was easy to miss. I scoped the problem, then designed and engineered Lenses end-to-end: a Chrome extension that reviews any deck in place, flags what matters to whoever’s using it, and stays grounded in Thresh’s own materials.
