01Problem

The internal AI video-editing tool built for the FIND Program was not working for its researchers.

The tool was meant to increase the speed at which researchers could dissect sessions and produce lab-standardized coaching videos, but inconsistency in the beta interface and distrust for AI-led tools led to abandonment across the research team.

02Methodology

Moderated Usability Testing

Unassisted, task-based sessions on the beta tool

30usability tests
What I did

Ran 30 moderated usability tests. Each user completed the central task on their own, from a raw clip all the way to downloading the lab-structured final.

I didn't answer questions. When someone asked for help on a part, I told them to think about how they would do it themselves, which showed where the intuition gaps were.

Why moderated

The lower fidelity of the beta left room for insights on both functionality and visual design. Going unassisted let us see how a user moves through the whole task and where intuition breaks down.

Digital Prototyping in Figma

Added to how we delivered insights to the software team

4participants per iteration, down from 12
What I did

Created and maintained a live Figma prototype to pass alongside insights to the software team, after noticing the gap in visual communication. We had no design team, and their process was to read what we gave them, picture it, then build it.

I introduced it after realizing that stacks of insights in words were hard for the team to sift through while still pushing new iterations on a quick turnaround.

Impact

It cut participants per iteration from 12 to 4. The back half of the initial 12 was giving repetitive feedback, so we got what we needed more quickly.

Post-Study Interview

Insights from the testing experience itself

Key insight

For almost every user, the interviews revealed distrust in the AI's context and capabilities. The primary frustration came from the platform positioning AI as the editor rather than an assistive form alongside the editing process.

What I did with it

I delivered that insight to the software team and communicated that users wanted more control and autonomy.

"I don't want AI to do everything for me if it's quick and I can fix it myself."

03Biggest impact from insight

Interface and product changes from usability testing led to the product being widely adopted across all FIND researchers.

Other contributions

01Research ops

Research operations and repository

Created and maintained a central repository for testing materials, session videos, notes, and insights; the live joint testing calendar; and the notetaking standard for consistency across notes passed to software.

02Content design

Participant onboarding graphics

Created graphics to go with the introductory terms given to participants, after seeing a pattern of confusion about key lab terms that affected their ability to understand and complete the task.

03Content design

Content design across study materials

Managed content design across study materials, keeping lab terminology consistent, especially in pre-study introductory presentations and the usability study script.
