Thresh Consulting · Internal Tool

Lenses

Feedback on your deck from any lens, right as you build it.

Across functions, the people building Thresh decks all wanted the same thing: a clear, cohesive story, kept clear from the first slide through the last. Checking it meant stopping work, scrolling back, and reading the whole deck aloud, again and again. Feedback was siloed too. A designer catches brand and design issues but is out of their depth on business or PM concerns, and the reverse is just as true.

I scoped that problem, then designed and engineered Lenses end-to-end. It holds Thresh’s training and context for every function, founders included, so you can ask for feedback through any lens (that’s the name). It runs while you build and applies its suggestions in place, so the story stays clear from the jump and you never break your flow.
